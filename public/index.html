Questions

Good to know before we talk.

What does Groviadigi do?

Groviadigi builds custom CRMs, internal tools, workflow automations and business websites. The goal is to remove repetitive manual work, such as copying data between spreadsheets, chasing follow-ups and building reports by hand.

Does my business need automation or custom software?

Not always. We first review the process. Sometimes fixing the process is enough, sometimes only a few key steps need automating, and sometimes the full workflow can run on its own. You get an honest recommendation on the first call.

How long does a custom CRM or automation project take?

A small automation can go live in a few days. A custom CRM, internal tool or website usually takes three to eight weeks, depending on scope. You receive a written scope, timeline and quote before any work starts.

How much does custom software or automation cost?

The Missed Follow-up Fix costs ₹19,999 (₹14,999 for our first 5 clients), with your money back if your team isn't using it after 30 days. A custom CRM starts from ₹90,000 and is paid in three milestones. Care plans start at ₹5,000 a month. Every project gets a fixed written quote before any payment.

Can you work with the tools we already use?

Yes. We connect to tools you already use, such as Google Sheets, WhatsApp, email, forms and existing CRMs, instead of asking you to replace everything.

What happens after launch?

Every project ends with deployment, a handover with training and documentation, and an optional monthly care plan for fixes, updates and improvements.

Zapier vs custom code automation: which is cheaper?

For anything that runs often, custom code usually costs less over time. Zapier, Make and n8n charge a subscription that grows with every task. Groviadigi writes the automation in code on official APIs, so you pay once for the build and then only for hosting and any paid API usage.

Is my data safe with your automations?

Your data moves directly between your own apps through their official APIs, without passing through a third-party automation platform. API keys are stored securely, each automation gets only the access it needs, and the code lives in your own account.

Custom CRM or Zoho / HubSpot: which is better for a small business?

If your sales process fits a standard CRM, use one; we can set it up. A custom CRM makes sense when you keep working around the software, pay for seats you don’t use, or need steps like site surveys, quotations or milestone payments built in.

Do you work with businesses outside India?

Yes. We work with teams in India, the US, the UK and the UAE, with calls scheduled in your time zone.
