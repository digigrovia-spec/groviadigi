Questions

Good to know before we talk.

What does Groviadigi do?

We build custom CRMs, automations, internal tools and websites. The goal is simple: stop your team doing the same manual work again and again, like copying data between sheets, chasing follow-ups or making reports by hand.

Does my business need automation or custom software?

Not always. We look at your process first. Sometimes a small change is enough. Sometimes only a few steps need automating. Sometimes the whole process can run by itself. You get an honest answer on the first call.

How long does a custom CRM or automation project take?

A small automation can be ready in a few days. A custom CRM, internal tool or website usually takes 3 to 8 weeks. You get a written plan, timeline and price before any work starts.

How much does custom software or automation cost?

Our starter offer, Follow-up Reminders (automatic WhatsApp reminders for your sales team), costs ₹19,999 (₹14,999 for our first 5 clients), with a full refund if your team isn’t using it after 30 days. A custom CRM starts at ₹90,000, paid in three parts. Care plans start at ₹5,000 a month. You always get a fixed price in writing before you pay.

Can you work with the tools we already use?

Yes. We work with what you already use, like Google Sheets, WhatsApp, email, forms and your current CRM. You don’t have to replace everything.

What happens after launch?

We launch it, train your team and give you simple guides. After that, you can choose a monthly care plan for fixes, updates and improvements.

Zapier vs custom code automation: which is cheaper?

If it runs often, custom code is usually cheaper over time. Zapier, Make and n8n charge a monthly fee that goes up the more you use them. We write the automation in code, so you pay once to build it, then only a small hosting cost.

Is my data safe with your automations?

Yes. Your data goes straight between your own apps, not through another company’s platform. Access keys are stored safely, each automation only gets the access it needs, and the code stays in your account.

Custom CRM or Zoho / HubSpot: which is better for a small business?

If a standard CRM fits how you sell, use it; we can set it up for you. A custom CRM makes sense when you keep working around your software, pay for users you don’t need, or need steps like site visits, quotes or part payments built in.

Do you work with businesses outside India?

Yes. We work with businesses in India, the US, the UK and the UAE, and schedule calls in your time zone.
