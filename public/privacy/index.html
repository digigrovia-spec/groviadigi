Privacy and cookies

Last updated 16 November 2026. This page explains what Groviadigi collects on groviadigi.in, why, and how to change your choice.

In short

We collect the details you type into our forms so we can reply to you. With your permission, we also use analytics and advertising cookies to see which pages help visitors and to measure our ads. We do not sell your data. You can change your cookie choice at any time with the button below or the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of every page.

Who we are

Groviadigi is a business automation and custom software studio in Ajmer, Rajasthan, India. For any privacy question, write to hello@groviadigi.in.

What you send us through a form

When you fill in a form, we receive what you type: name, email, phone, company, the services you picked, budget, start date and your message. We also record the page you sent it from and, if you came from an ad or link, its campaign tags. These details go to a private Google Sheet that only our team can open. We use them to reply to your request and to prepare for your call. We do not add you to a mailing list without asking.

Cookies we use

Necessary cookies are always on. Analytics and marketing cookies load only after you allow them in the cookie banner. Until then, Google tools run in a limited mode that does not store cookies.

Your cookie choice is recorded

When you choose, we save a record with a random ID, what you allowed, the date and the version of this policy. It is stored in your browser for 12 months and, for our records, in our private Google Sheet. The record does not include your name, email or IP address. After 12 months, or if our cookie list changes, we ask again.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the details you sent through a form. Write to hello@groviadigi.in and we will reply within 30 days. You can also withdraw cookie consent at any time; tools you switch off stop loading on your next page view.

How long we keep form details

We keep enquiry details for as long as we are talking about a project, and for up to 3 years after that so we have a record of what was agreed. Ask us and we will delete them sooner.
