Privacy Policy and Cookies

Last updated 26 November 2026. This policy explains what personal data Groviadigi collects on groviadigi.in, why, who we share it with, and the rights you have.

In short

We collect the details you send through our forms so we can reply to you, and nothing else without asking. Analytics and advertising cookies load only if you allow them. We do not sell your data. You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time by writing to hello@groviadigi.in.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Groviadigi, a business automation and custom software studio based in Ajmer, Rajasthan, India, decides how your personal data is used. Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) we are the Data Fiduciary, and under the EU and UK GDPR we are the controller.

Contact and grievance officer: Grievance Officer, Groviadigi, Ajmer, Rajasthan, India · email hello@groviadigi.in · phone or WhatsApp +91 93520 00935. We reply within 30 days.

2. What we collect

  • What you send us in a form: first and last name, work email, company name, WhatsApp number and country code, country (optional), the services you are interested in, when you want to start and your message.
  • Where you came from: the page you used, the website that sent you, and campaign tags in the link (for example utm_source).
  • Cookie choice: a random ID, the choice you made and the date (see section 6).
  • With your consent only: analytics and advertising data described in section 6.
  • Technical logs: our hosting provider keeps short logs, such as IP address and browser type, to keep the site running and secure.

We do not ask for sensitive data such as health, financial account or identity numbers through this website. Please do not send them in a form.

3. Why we use it, and the legal basis

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare for your call. DPDP Act: data you gave us voluntarily for this purpose (a legitimate use under section 7). GDPR: steps you asked for before a contract, and our legitimate interest in replying.
  • To measure and improve the website and our ads. Only with your consent through the cookie banner (DPDP Act section 6; GDPR consent). You can withdraw it at any time.
  • To keep records of our agreements and meet legal duties, such as tax and accounting rules.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for automated decisions that have legal or similar effects on you.

4. Who we share it with

Only with the service providers that run our website and tools, under their data protection terms. They may store data outside India.

5. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details while we are discussing a project, and for up to 3 years afterwards so we have a record of what was agreed. If no project starts, we delete them within 12 months of our last contact, or sooner if you ask. Cookie choices are kept for 12 months.

6. Cookies

Necessary cookies are always on. Analytics and marketing cookies load only after you allow them in the cookie banner, and “Only necessary” is as easy to choose as “Accept all”. Until you choose, Google tools run in a limited mode that stores no cookies. Your choice is saved so we do not ask again for 12 months.

7. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act and the GDPR you can:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • ask us to correct, complete, update or delete it;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (through “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page, or by writing to us);
  • object to our use of your data, or ask us to limit it (GDPR);
  • receive your data in a common format (GDPR);
  • nominate someone to act for you if you die or cannot act (DPDP Act);
  • complain to us first, through the grievance officer in section 1.

Write to hello@groviadigi.in. We may ask you to confirm who you are. We reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India, or, in the EU or UK, to your local data protection authority.

8. Security

We limit who can see personal data, use secure connections (HTTPS) and keep access to our tools protected. No website can be fully secure, so if we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Children

This website is for businesses. It is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has sent us data, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our tools or the law change, and show the new date at the top. If a change affects cookies, we will ask for your choice again.
