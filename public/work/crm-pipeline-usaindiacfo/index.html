Executive summary

USAIndiaCFO’s leads come from cold email, the website, WhatsApp and paid ads, and each one has to land in one place with a clear status. Groviadigi set up the system around Zoho CRM: a six-stage pipeline from sourcing to reporting, CRM workflows and exports, shared inboxes, WhatsApp through the Cloud API, and the outbound setup that feeds it, with 10 domains, 20 sending mailboxes and automated sequences.

The challenge

  1. Leads from five directionsCold email, website, WhatsApp, Meta and LinkedIn ads, each with its own inbox or dashboard.
  2. Shared inboxesSeveral teams answer from shared inboxes, risking duplicate deliveries and missed replies.
  3. Deliverability at volume10,000+ emails a month needs authenticated domains and warmed mailboxes.
  4. Limited data creditsNo contact should be bought twice.
  5. No single status per leadNobody could see at a glance who was contacted, who replied and who signed.

What we built

01Zoho CRM workflowsWorkflow rules and exports in the CRM every lead ends up in.
02Pipeline syncOutbound activity syncs into the CRM, next to every other lead.
03Shared inboxesTeam inboxes set up, with duplicate-delivery issues investigated.
04WhatsApp Cloud APIConnected to the firm’s WhatsApp Business account.
05Sending domains10 domains with SPF, DKIM and DMARC, plus pre-warmed bridge mailboxes.
06Remarketing on CRM dataA 90-day plan across email, WhatsApp, Meta, LinkedIn and Google.
