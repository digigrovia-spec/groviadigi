Executive summary

USAIndiaCFO already ran its sales on Zoho CRM. What it lacked was a steady flow of new, qualified leads and a way to see each lead’s status. We built the outbound automation that finds likely clients, removes duplicates, enriches each lead, sends cold email from authenticated mailboxes and writes every outcome back into the firm’s Zoho CRM. We did not build the CRM itself; we connected our system to it.

The challenge

  1. Not enough new leadsFinding and qualifying new clients by hand took hours each week.
  2. Leads in many placesCold email, the website, WhatsApp and ads each kept their own lists.
  3. Deliverability at volumeSending more than 10,000 emails a month needs authenticated domains and warmed mailboxes, or mail goes to spam.
  4. Limited data creditsLead data costs money, so no contact should be bought twice.
  5. No single status per leadNobody could see at a glance who was contacted, who replied and who signed.

What we built

01Lead findingThe automation finds likely clients for each audience segment and gathers their details.
02Duplicate checkEvery new contact is checked against the master list first, so no one is bought or emailed twice.
03Lead enrichmentEach lead gets a short profile: what the company does and what is worth pitching, ready before the first call.
04Cold email sending20 mailboxes on 10 domains with SPF, DKIM and DMARC, warmed up before volume went up.
05Zoho CRM integrationEvery reply, meeting and outcome is written into the firm’s existing Zoho CRM, next to its other leads.
06Reports and remarketingWeekly and monthly reports, plus a remarketing plan built from the CRM’s own records.
