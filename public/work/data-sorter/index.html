Executive summary

The client, a cross-border CFO firm, runs cold email and paid campaigns on Meta and LinkedIn from the same pool of contacts. Lists arrive from Apollo, past campaigns and the ad platforms, and without one master list every new file risks duplicates and repeat contact. Data Sorter keeps one master database, flags duplicates and already-used contacts on upload, builds segments, and exports a clean file for each channel while marking those contacts as used.

The challenge

  1. Lists from many sourcesApollo exports, re-enriched past campaigns, and Meta and LinkedIn ad data.
  2. Duplicates across filesThe same person could sit in two or three lists, and every copy cost time or budget.
  3. Repeat contactWithout a usage record, a channel could contact someone it had already used.
  4. Limited data creditsApollo credits are limited, so recycled contacts had to be reused with care.
  5. Segments to keep fullEight segments need 1,750 to 3,000 new sequence-ready contacts every month.
