Executive summary

USAIndiaCFO’s marketing and sales work ran in separate places, and the founder had no single view of what was working. We built Growth Center: four automations for social media, search, outbound email and paid ads, each sending its results to one central dashboard. The data is stored on an in-house server we built, which has no running cost. A person approves every action before it goes live, and each automation learns from those reviews. Since launch, the firm’s sales have grown by 15%.

The challenge

  1. Growth work in separate placesSocial media, search, outreach and ads each ran in their own tools, so results were hard to compare.
  2. No single view for the founderSeeing leads converted, client work status or SEO progress meant asking different people.
  3. Repeat work every dayCreatives, posts, blogs, outreach emails and ad checks took the team’s time every week.
  4. Client data had to stay safeA finance firm cannot risk its data leaving its own systems.

What we built

01Growth Center dashboardOne screen where the founder sees each department’s results: leads converted, client work done and pending, SEO, marketing, posting and the latest blogs.
021. Social media automationCreatives, short videos and post copy are made automatically. Replies to comments and messages are drafted by AI, and real enquiries go straight to the sales team.
032. SEO, AEO and GEO automationA content calendar, blogs written and published to the website, analytics, a month-end report and regular website maintenance. Website changes can be made from the dashboard.
043. Outbound automationFinds likely clients, gathers their details and sends cold emails, then records every reply.
054. Performance marketing automationRuns ads, analyses how they perform and suggests changes. Nothing changes until a person approves it.
06In-house server, one-way dataEvery automation sends its data to a server we built for the firm. Data flows in but never flows out, so even if something harmful got in, it would have no way to send data out.
07Access by roleEach person sees only the dashboards and automations their role needs, from team member up to the founder.
08Lead enrichment before every callWhen a lead enters the CRM, a short brief is ready before the first call: what the company does, what to pitch and what to avoid.

How it works

How we delivered it

  1. Map the workEach department’s daily tasks and the results the founder wanted to see.
  2. Build the four automationsSocial media, search, outbound and performance marketing, one at a time.
  3. Build Growth CenterThe central dashboard, the in-house server and access by role.
  4. Add human approvalReview steps before any post, email or ad change goes out.
  5. Learn and improveEach automation learns from the team’s approvals and edits.
