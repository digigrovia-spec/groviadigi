Growth Center: a search dashboard that plans its own month

An automated dashboard that drafts a monthly strategy, gets one human review, then runs research, writing, fact-checking, publishing and reporting every day.

Executive summary

To rank for US and India tax and compliance topics, on Google and inside AI answers, USAIndiaCFO needs steady, accurate content and constant tracking. Growth Center drafts a monthly strategy, sends it for one human review, and then runs the month: daily topic research, writing, fact-checking, publishing and reporting. Human input is about 1% of the work, mostly that monthly review.

The challenge

  1. Search has three frontsBuyers search on Google, read direct answers and ask AI assistants; each needs its own work.
  2. Rules change oftenTax and compliance rules and deadlines change through the year, so topics must stay current.
  3. Accuracy is not optionalA wrong deadline in a tax article damages trust, so every piece needs a fact-check.
  4. Too many daily stepsResearch, writing, review, publishing and reporting every day adds up to a lot of manual work.
  5. Reporting in different toolsRankings, answer-box visibility and AI mentions sat in separate tools.

What we built

01Monthly strategyThe dashboard drafts the month’s plan with one goal: grow SEO, AEO and GEO visibility.
02Daily topic researchAt 10:00 AM IST it reviews the last two days of news and rule changes and suggests up to five sourced topics.
03Monthly calendarPlanned blogs sit on a calendar, with drafts prepared 72 hours before each slot.
04Research, draft, fact-checkBlogs and LinkedIn articles go through one workflow that ends in a fact-check.
05Review and publishDrafts wait in a review queue for a set window, then publish on schedule.
06ReportsOverall, SEO, AEO and GEO views, with analytics and a month-end report.

How it works

  1. Dashboard drafts the month
  2. A person reviews it
  3. System runs the month

How we delivered it

  1. Choose the toolsOne tool per job: rank tracking, on-page scoring, Search Console and GA4.
  2. Build the dashboardA web app on Vercel with a Postgres database.
  3. Connect the toolsResearch, writing and reporting share the same data.
  4. Strategy and reportsMonthly strategy, analytics and month-end reporting.
  5. HandoverEvery account and setting documented for day-to-day running.

Why it worked

One goal per monthEvery task traces back to a single goal: grow SEO, AEO and GEO visibility.
People decide, the system worksA person approves or edits the plan; the dashboard does the daily work.
Facts checked firstEvery draft passes a fact-check, which matters in tax content.
