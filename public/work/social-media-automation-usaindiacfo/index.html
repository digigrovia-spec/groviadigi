Executive summary

Every blog needed a banner, every update needed a post, and on busy weeks the look drifted. We built a social media automation for USAIndiaCFO that makes creatives, short videos and post copy in one fixed brand kit, drafts replies to comments and messages, and sends real enquiries to the sales team. A person approves what goes out, and the system learns from each review.

The challenge

  1. Too many creatives to makeBanners, posts and videos for every blog and update took a designer’s week.
  2. The brand driftedWhen the team rushed, fonts, colours and layouts changed from post to post.
  3. Replies and enquiries were slowComments and messages waited, and real enquiries could be missed.

What we built

01One brand kit, defined onceFixed colours, fonts and layouts, with contrast checked in code so text stays readable.
02Creatives, videos and copyBanners for every blog, posts for every update and short videos, made in the house style.
03AI-drafted repliesReplies to comments and messages are drafted automatically; real enquiries go straight to the sales team.
04Learns from examplesThe team picks design videos it likes; the system turns them into design rules it follows.
05Memory of mistakesCorrections are remembered, so the same mistake is less likely to come back.
06Linked to search and Growth CenterShares topics and designs with the SEO automation and reports results to Growth Center.

How it works

  1. Plan from the content calendar
  2. Make creatives and copy
  3. A person approves
  4. Post and reply
  5. Results go to Growth Center
