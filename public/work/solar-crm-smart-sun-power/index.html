Executive summary

Before the CRM, Smart Sun Power ran its whole sales and project pipeline across WhatsApp chats, phone calls, spreadsheets and scattered files. Follow-ups were missed, quotations went untracked and management had little visibility. The custom solar CRM brings lead management, follow-ups, site surveys, quotations, installation tracking and payments into one connected platform, so the business sees its whole pipeline in real time.

The challenge

  1. Fragmented lead managementLeads arrived through WhatsApp, calls and the website, with no single place to see them.
  2. Missed follow-upsWith no task list or reminders, follow-ups depended on memory and some leads went cold.
  3. Disorganised documentsSurvey notes, quotations and customer files were spread across chats, email and folders.
  4. No tracking after the saleOnce a deal closed, nobody could see where procurement, installation and commissioning stood.
  5. Low management visibilityPipeline value, conversion and payments were hard to see without asking around.

What we built

01Lead management dashboardLeads from the website, calls and WhatsApp referrals land in one place, each with a sales owner and a tracked source.
02Automated follow-upsThe team logs calls and sets next actions; the system alerts them the moment a follow-up is due.
03Site surveys and quotationsSurveys move from scheduled to completed; quotation templates fill in lead and site data and go from draft to final.
04Installation trackingClosed deals move straight into installation, with milestones from material procurement to commissioning.
05Payments and collectionsAdvance, on-installation and on-completion milestones with due dates, alerts and linked receipts.
06Executive dashboardPipeline value, conversion, sales-cycle length, team performance, profitability and payment status in real time.
