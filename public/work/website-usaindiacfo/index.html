Executive summary

USAIndiaCFO handles virtual CFO, tax and compliance work for businesses and families across the US and India. Its old website ran on WordPress, with search settings handled by a plugin. Groviadigi designed and built a new site from scratch: three practice hubs with 54 service pages, site-wide search, a booking flow for discovery calls, and the SEO basics written into the code. It went live in October 2026, ahead of the deadline.

The challenge

  1. SEO tied to a pluginSearch settings lived in a WordPress plugin, so on a custom site SEO had to be built into the code.
  2. 54 services to organiseFrom US incorporation and ITIN to GST and FEMA compliance, in a structure a first-time visitor can follow.
  3. Two countries, two time zonesProspects in India and the US needed to reach the right team during their own working hours.
  4. Trust before the first callA finance firm has to show reviews, client names and the people behind the work early.
  5. A home for guidesTax and compliance guides needed a section readers and search engines could find easily.

What we built

01Practice hubsUSA CFO Services, India CFO Services and Family Office, each with an overview and its own list of services.
0254 service pages18 for the USA, 23 for India and 13 for the Family Office, each with its own page and URL.
03Site-wide searchCovers services, pages and Insights articles; press the / key to open it.
04Discovery-call bookingThe scheduler loads only after a click, which keeps pages light, with email as a fallback.
05Contact in two time zonesUS and India phone lines with office hours in EST and IST, plus WhatsApp.
06Search and policy basicsCanonical URLs, meta descriptions and share images, plus privacy, terms and accessibility pages.
